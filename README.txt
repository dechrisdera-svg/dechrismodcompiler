DechrisModCompiler SEO deployment files

Files:
- index.html: updated homepage with SEO metadata, structured data, visible logo and favicon references.
- robots.txt: allows public crawling and points crawlers to the sitemap.
- sitemap.xml: sitemap for the public homepage.

Important:
1. Upload your logo image as /logo.png at the site root (same level as index.html).
2. Deploy all files to the root of the Vercel site.
3. Verify these URLs after deployment:
   https://dechrismodcompiler.vercel.app/robots.txt
   https://dechrismodcompiler.vercel.app/sitemap.xml
   https://dechrismodcompiler.vercel.app/logo.png
4. In Google Search Console, add/verify the property and submit sitemap.xml.
5. Search indexing and ranking are not guaranteed; Google decides when and what to index.

The existing page layout and styling are retained, apart from the small header logo treatment.
